Features

Multiple Characters

No more one-size-fits-all narration. Each story can be voiced with multiple distinct characters, giving dialogue depth and authenticity. Listeners can instantly recognize who’s speaking, while authors gain a powerful tool for making their worlds more immersive.

Multiple Voices

Flexibility meets creativity. Choose from a variety of voices that fit the tone and personality of your work — from epic narrators to subtle conversational tones. This ensures every story has the perfect soundscape, tailored to the author’s vision.

Character Emotions

Emotions make stories unforgettable. With expressive delivery, characters can laugh, cry, shout, or whisper with genuine feeling. Authors can control the mood, while listeners experience narration that captures the heart of every scene.

Targeted Reruns

Perfecting narration doesn’t mean starting from scratch. With targeted re-runs, you can instantly replay specific sections of text — whether it’s a single line of dialogue or an entire scene. Want to try it with a different voice? Add a touch more emotion? No problem. Authors can fine-tune delivery on demand, making sure every moment sounds exactly how they imagined.

Audio Store

Publishing doesn’t end with the written word. Our built-in audio store provides new ways for authors to share and monetize their work. Fans can support creators directly, explore unique audio experiences, and even collect the stories they love most.

Community Involvement

Storytelling is no longer one-directional. Fans can become part of the creative journey by suggesting ideas, influencing outcomes, or engaging with content in exciting new ways — always with the author’s guidance. This makes every project a collaboration between creator and community.
